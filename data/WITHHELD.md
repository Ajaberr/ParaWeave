# Withheld Arabic articles

Ten of the 2,330 Arabic articles contain sentences from the Multidialectal Arabic Parallel Corpus (MDC; Bouamor et al., 2014),
whose licence permits internal research use only and no redistribution. These articles, their summaries, claims and verdicts,
their anchor and translation rows and the two survey items built on them are withheld from this release; the paper's
results include them. Researchers holding the MDC licence can obtain them from the authors.
